A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING
Is ITS3LF a store?+

No. ITS3LF is a platform that works with participating stores to help you scan products, manage your basket and pay during an in-store visit.

Can I use it at any store?+

You can use ITS3LF at participating stores available in the app. Choose your store before you start scanning products.

Can I pay with cash?+

Yes. Choose cash and show your payment QR at the store counter. Staff will process your payment before you complete the exit check.
